THE FIELD GUIDE

A little truth.
A lot of bluff.

  1. Make something up. Use the host’s bluff timer to write a believable fake answer.
  2. Find the truth. Use the host’s vote timer to pick the real answer. Your own bluff is never eligible.
  3. Watch the reveal. Earn 1,000 for the truth and 500 for each opponent you fool.
  4. Take the crown. The highest score after the selected 3, 5, 7, or 10 rounds wins. Ties share the crown.

Two players? Each gets the truth, their opponent’s bluff, and game-written decoys to make four choices. With more players, we add decoys only when needed.

Recognized correct submissions earn 1,000 truth points at the reveal and skip voting. Identical bluffs are merged; every author earns bluff points. Missed actions earn no points. Players joining mid-match wait for the rematch.

500 questions: 150 funny facts, 150 tricky questions, 100 general knowledge, and 100 riddles or word puzzles. A room never repeats a question until its host explicitly resets an exhausted bank. New rooms have separate histories.

The host chooses rounds, bluff/vote timers, and 0–7 bots. Bots use curated bluffs and random eligible votes; they do not use a generative AI service. You can practise alone with bots. Settings are locked while a match runs.

Use your imagination, not a search engine. Keep answers friendly.
